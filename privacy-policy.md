# Privacy Policy

**Hangman Chalk: Spelling**
Last updated: 21 August 2026

## The short version

Hangman Chalk does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There
are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any
kind. Everything the app remembers about you stays on your device.

This app is designed for children, and it is built so that there is nothing to
leak.

## What stays on your device

The app saves a few things locally so the game works between sessions:

- **Game progress** — which words you have completed in each pack.
- **Player statistics** — wins, streaks, and which achievements you have earned.
- **Settings** — sound on or off, difficulty, Kids Mode, and the chosen avatar.
- **The robot's learning data** — the letter patterns the computer opponent has
  picked up from play.
- **Player names** — if you type a name for pass-and-play, it is stored on the
  device only.

This information is never uploaded to us. We do not operate a server, and there
is no account to sign into. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Apple's services

Three features use Apple's own services. In each case the data goes to Apple
under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not to us,
and we never receive it.

- **Game Center** — if you are signed in, achievements are recorded against your
  Game Center account so they appear across your devices. Game Center is not
  used when Kids Mode is on: the app does not prompt a child to sign in.
- **In-app purchases** — handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see
  your payment details. In a Family Sharing group, a child's purchase is sent to
  a parent or guardian for approval (Ask to Buy).
- **Online play and iMessage** — if you play a game with someone else, the word
  and the letters guessed are sent to that person so the game can be played.
  Nothing is stored by us, and there is no chat, profile, or message feature.

## Age assurance

On operating systems that support it, the app asks the system whether the user is
a child, and if so turns Kids Mode on and locks it. It receives only an age
range from the OS — never a date of birth, a name, or any identifier.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect personal information.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- We do not show advertising.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

## Children's privacy

This app is intended for use by children as well as adults, and it is designed
to comply with the App Store's Kids Category requirements and with children's
privacy laws including COPPA and the UK GDPR. Because the app collects no
personal information at all, there is nothing that requires parental consent to
collect, and no data that could be disclosed to a third party.
