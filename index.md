# Hangman Chalk: Spelling

A chalkboard word game with **1,567 illustrated words** and a robot opponent
that learns how you play.

Every word has a hand-drawn picture and a fact card. Word packs follow what
children are actually taught — Reception sight words, the Key Stage 1 list, and
the full Key Stage 2 statutory spelling list — alongside packs for the curious
and a few for grown-ups.

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. The whole game works offline.

Available for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac.
