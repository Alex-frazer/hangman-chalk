# Hangman Chalk — Support

A chalkboard word game with 1,567 illustrated words and a robot opponent that
learns as you play.

## Contact

Email **alex_frazer_485@hotmail.com** and I'll get back to you. If you're reporting a
problem, it helps to include your device, the iOS/tvOS/macOS version, and what
you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

### I bought a pack but it hasn't appeared

Open the **Word Shop** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Purchases are tied to your
Apple Account, so signing in with a different one will not show them.

### My child's purchase is waiting for approval

That's **Ask to Buy**, part of Apple's Family Sharing. The purchase pauses until
a parent or guardian approves it from their own device, and the pack unlocks
automatically once they do. Nothing is charged before approval.

### The Word Shop says "no word packs available"

This usually means the device can't reach the App Store. Check the connection
and reopen the shop. If it persists, restarting the app will retry.

### What is Kids Mode?

It hides the grown-up word packs from both the category list and the shop,
leaving the packs written for younger readers. On systems that support it, the
app asks the OS whether the user is a child and turns Kids Mode on
automatically, locking it so it can't be switched off.

### Which packs suit which age?

- **First Words**, **Animal Friends**, **Colours**, **Numbers**, **My Body** —
  first readers
- **Sight Words (Reception)**, **Spelling Stars (KS1)** — Reception and Key
  Stage 1
- **Spelling Champions (KS2)** — the Key Stage 2 statutory spelling list
- **Science Starters**, **World Explorer**, **Dinosaur Discovery**,
  **Ocean Friends**, **Bugs & Butterflies** — curious readers
- **Capital Cities**, **The Elements**, **Dog Breeds**, **Cat Breeds**,
  **Countries** — older children and adults

### Does it work offline?

Yes. Every word, illustration and fact card is included in the app, and the
robot opponent runs on the device. Only buying packs and online play need a
connection.

### Achievements aren't recording

Achievements need Game Center. Check you're signed in under **Settings → Game
Center**. In Kids Mode the app deliberately doesn't prompt for sign-in;
progress is still saved on the device and reports to Game Center once an adult
plays signed in.

### How do I turn the sound off?

**Settings** inside the app. The same switch controls the chalk sounds and the
vibration feedback.
